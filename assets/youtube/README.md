Welcome to Bike Setup Tracker — the free mobile app for cyclists who want to stop guessing and start tracking their settings with precision.

Whether you're a downhill racer chasing the perfect suspension tune, an enduro rider adapting to changing trail conditions, or a road cyclist obsessing over tire pressure and fit — this channel shows you how to get the most out of your equipment.

What you'll find here:

- Tips for tracking suspension, tires, geometry & more
