## Description (527 characters)
```text
Bike Setup Tracker is a free logbook for cyclists. Keep every bike and component in a garage, log your setup for each ride (fork and shock pressure, rebound, sag, tire pressure, or anything else you adjust) and let the app add the weather and location. The history shows exactly what changed between setups, charts show how each setting evolved, and service tasks remind you when a component is due. No account, no ads, data stored on the device, export to Excel, CSV or JSON. Optional paid Strava Sync matches setups to rides.
```
