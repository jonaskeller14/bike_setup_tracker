# Showcase kit posts

Drafts for `build_kit.py 02_showcase` (#77 Phase 7), rendered into the "Posts" part of
`build/marketing/02_showcase/POST.md` with campaign links, the app version and character counts.
Format and placeholders: [`tool/marketing/posts.py`](../../../tool/marketing/posts.py). Posting rules:
[`../channels.md`](../channels.md). Hooks and headlines come from
[`../messaging.md`](../messaging.md).

The kit is evergreen: it doesn't announce a release. Every Short starts with a question hook, so
the post text answers it instead of repeating it.

Facts every post may state (checked against release build 1.6.3): free core app, no account, no
ads, data stored on the phone, export to Excel/CSV/JSON, weather and location filled in
automatically, suggested settings when adding a fork or shock, setup history with old → new values,
compare two setups, line and radial charts per setting. Tasks and the calendar are on in
Settings → Features. Strava Sync is the only paid part: rides on the map, component km and
km-based service intervals need it. Never mention presets, ratings, pressure check, riders or
bookmarks, and never claim the app finds, recommends or tunes a setting (messaging.md, Is / is not).
