# 04_v1.6.4 kit posts

Drafts for `build_kit.py 04_v1.6.4` ([plan](../plan_v1.6.4.md), batch 1), rendered into
`build/marketing/04_v1.6.4/posts/`. Format and placeholders: [`tool/marketing/posts.py`](../../../tool/marketing/posts.py).
Posting rules: [`../channels.md`](../channels.md). Hooks come from [`../messaging.md`](../messaging.md).

Release posts go out only once 1.6.4 is live in **both** stores (plan, Back-planning). Their base is
the "What's New" text `docs/store/play-store/metadata/en-US/changelogs/45.txt`, without the value
migration line (plan: not in release content).

Facts every post may state (checked against release build 45): the fork and shock catalog
(Component Presets, on by default): pick brand, model, year and travel, and the adjusters are
filled in with their click ranges, air pressure and sag; 14 brands. Service tasks with the
manufacturer's intervals when adding a catalog part (Task Presets, on by default, but Tasks itself
is off: say "Switch on Tasks under Settings → Features" once, in the description of the posts that
show them). Dial color and size in one sheet. Restoring an old setup carries its values over to new
parts. Riding-hour intervals count Strava rides: Strava Sync is the paid add-on. Never mention
ratings, feedback, pressure check, setup guides, riders or bookmarks, and never claim the app finds,
recommends or tunes a setting: the catalog fills in what a part can do, not what to set. Every post
that shows or names a brand ends with the disclaimer (`channels.md`).
