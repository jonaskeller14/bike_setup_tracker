# 04_v1.6.4: voiceover scripts

Voiceover for the four feature Shorts of kit `04_v1.6.4` ([plan](../plan_v1.6.4.md), N1, N2, N8, N5),
voice **Rowan HD** in [speechma](https://speechma.com). Batches, slates and pauses work as in
[`03_features.vo.md`](03_features.vo.md): the text is
`tool/marketing/assets/voice/04_v1.6.4/batch-N.md` (tracked; paste it as is), the audio `batch-N.mp3`
next to it (gitignored).

## Style

The rules of [`03_features.vo.md`](03_features.vo.md#style): the hook word for word, then the scenario,
the action, the payoff; simple words; every spoken number from the sample or the catalog. Opt-in
features (Tasks) and Strava Sync being paid go in the post text, not in the voice. The catalog
fills in what a part can do (ranges, intervals), never what to set (messaging.md, Is / is not).

### Facts behind the scripts

- `catalog`: RockShox Lyrik Ultimate, 2025–2026, Charger 3.1 RC2 (`data/component_presets/fork/rockshox.yaml`):
  Rebound 0–18 clicks, LSC 15 positions (−7…+7), HSC 5 (−2…+2). The flow folds the catalog notes
  away, because they link the manufacturer's setup guide.
- `tasks`: the same fork; the task sheet offers *Lower leg service* every 50 h and *Full service*
  every 200 h, "RockShox service intervals". Hours are riding time from Strava (paid); Tasks is
  opt-in. Both go in the post text.
- `dial`: scenario `dial_style`, the sample Lyrik with every dial in the default style. Rebound
  turns red (the RockShox rebound knob is red), LSC small (the smaller knob stacked on HSC,
  RockShox "Compression Knob Logic").
- `brands`: 14 brands a release build lists across forks and shocks (fork: FOX, RockShox, BOS,
  Cane Creek, Cannondale, DT Swiss, DVO, EXT, Formula, Manitou, Marzocchi, RST, SR SUNTOUR, Öhlins;
  shocks the same without Cannondale). Intend has a fork file, but every trim is a draft, so the
  picker hides it: the plan's "15 brands" counted it. RockShox Judy isn't in the catalog: the flow
  requests it.

## When listening

- "eighteen clicks", "fifteen": numbers, not "one eight"
- "Strava": stress on the first syllable
- Length: full takes 18–26 s; `brands` about 12 s
