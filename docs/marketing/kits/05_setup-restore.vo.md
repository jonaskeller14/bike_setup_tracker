# 05_setup-restore: voiceover script

Voiceover for the `restore` Short of kit `05_setup-restore`, voice **Rowan HD** in
[speechma](https://speechma.com). Batches, slates and pauses work as in
[`03_features.vo.md`](03_features.vo.md): the text is
`tool/marketing/assets/voice/05_setup-restore/batch-N.md` (tracked; paste it as is), the audio
`batch-N.mp3` next to it (gitignored).

## Style

The rules of [`03_features.vo.md`](03_features.vo.md#style): the hook word for word, then the scenario,
the action, the payoff; simple words. The rider decides which setup felt right; the app only keeps it
and brings its values back (messaging.md, Is / is not).

### Facts behind the script

- Scenario `restore`: the Hightower with four setups. Newest *Hometrails - firmer fork* (pressure
  84 → 88 psi, LSC 8 → 6, rebound 9 → 11; its note: "Harsh on the roots now"). Clicks count from
  closed, so fewer LSC clicks are firmer and more rebound clicks are faster, then *Finale Ligure -
  DH Men* ("Fork felt right"), which the flow restores from its tile's menu.
- The restore form shows the old values with what changed; the saved copy lists 88 → 84, 6 → 8 and
  11 → 9, and the old setup stays. It also gets today's date, place and weather (FAQ
  `faqSetupRestoreAnswer`), which the script leaves out as beside the point.
- No spoken numbers: they are on screen. "Compression" is the LSC row.
