# 07_v1.8.0: voiceover scripts

Voiceover for the Shorts of kit `07_v1.8.0`, the first kit in the Shorts formats (`brand.md`, Formats), voice
**Rowan HD** in [speechma](https://speechma.com). The text is `tool/marketing/assets/voice/07_v1.8.0/batch-N.md`
(tracked; paste it as is), the audio `batch-N.mp3` next to it (gitignored).

## Style

The rules in [`messaging.md`](../messaging.md#voiceover-scripts) (Voiceover scripts, 2026-11-15), with the hook word
for word from the bank. Batches 1–3 predate those rules and are not used; batches 4–6 are the rewrite. Each paragraph
is one caption line, and the formats time to them:

- **How to:** the hook, then the steps; the step number rolls on the paragraphs in the kit's `steps` (the switch is
  step 1 in the two opt-in How-tos), and the closing line stays on step 03.
- **Before / After:** the hook and paragraph 1 run over the note or the spec table; the page turns on paragraph 2.
- **For riders who…:** the hook over the rider word; the phone slides up on paragraph 1.

Setup Guides are "the manual's starting values" (`messaging.md`, Is / is not), even though the card's own heading
says "Recommended first values". Opt-in and paid notes go in the post text, not the voice.

### Facts behind the script

Read off the recordings in `build/marketing/raw/ios/` on 2026-11-13; Setup Guides and Pressure Check re-recorded
on 2026-11-15, after the guides keyed rebound on the entered pressure.

- **Setup Guides** (`demo_setup_guide`, scenario `one_bike_no_components`): the 2025–2026 Lyrik Ultimate from the
  catalog, 160 mm, riding weight 78 kg. The guide gives pressure 76 psi (71–81, interpolated), 0 volume spacers,
  HSC 0, LSC 0 and rebound 8 (7–8, from RockShox TrailHead's formula); after Apply all the rebound row reads
  "76 psi (≈ 78 kg) · follows your pressure", still 8. The manual's own table is in lb: 160–180 lb → 70–80 psi at
  160 mm (78 kg ≈ 172 lb), which the voice and the drawn table in `ba-guide` use.
- **Pressure Check** (`demo_pressure_check`, scenario `pressure_check`): the fork was last set to 84.0 psi in
  "Back to baseline" on 2026-11-02 at 20 °C and 320 m; on the 3 °C morning at the same altitude the pump would read
  78.5 psi (−6.6 %). The shock reads 182 against 192 psi. Tires are hidden in the scenario. Forks lose no air in the model,
  so "no air got out" holds; the difference is temperature alone.
- **Log a setup** (`demo_add_setup`): Finale Ligure, clear sky, dry; rebound 9, pressure 84 psi, "Finale Ligure -
  baseline".
- **Charts** (`demo_analysis`): the Lyrik's line chart with LSC picked from the legend, the radial chart and the
  activity histogram.
- **Notebook** (`demo_setup_history`): "Steep chute - fork tweak" shows pressure 84 → 85, LSC 7 → 8, rebound
  9 → 10; the comparison with "Back to baseline" shows 85 against 84 psi and LSC 8 against 7. The cut ends before
  the flow picks "New baseline", whose comparison shows the rider's body weight (riders are not shown).
- **Racers** (`demo_setup_restore`, scenario `restore`): "Firmer fork" (88 psi, LSC 6, rebound 11, "harsh on the
  roots") restored from "Finale Ligure - DH Men" ("calm on the rocks, plenty of grip"): 84 psi, LSC 8, rebound 9.
  Clicks count from closed, so LSC 8 → 6 is firmer and rebound 9 → 11 faster.
- **Home mechanics** (`demo_tasks`, scenario `tasks_one_due`): "Investigate BB creaking noise" ticked off, then
  "All caught up"; upcoming: tubeless sealant every 120 days, rear brake pads every 700 km, shock air-can service
  every 50 h. Km intervals need Strava Sync; Tasks are opt-in.
- **Downhill** (`demo_downhill`, scenario `full`): the Trek Session's history, then "Leogang - Worldcup Track
  (wet)" (9 °C, slight rain, muddy) against "Leogang - Speedster (dry)": Fox 40 pressure 80 → 82 psi, LSC
  −9 → −8, Fox DHX2 LSC −8 → −7, as the setup's note says ("closed the low-speed compression a click front and
  rear and added 2 psi in the fork").
