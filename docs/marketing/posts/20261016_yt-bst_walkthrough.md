## Description
```text
Never lose your suspension sweet spot. Bike Setup Tracker is a free logbook for your bike setup: fork and shock pressure, rebound, compression, sag, tire pressure, and anything else you adjust, with the weather and location of every ride. This walkthrough shows version 1.6.3.

Free on iOS and Android. No account, no ads.

What you can do
• Keep every bike and component in one garage
• Log a setup in seconds; weather and location fill in themselves
• See exactly what changed between setups, like "Rebound 9 → 10"
• Compare any two setups and watch each setting evolve in charts
• Plan services and see rides, setups and services in one calendar
• Strava Sync (optional paid add-on): see which setup you rode on every ride

Your data stays on your phone, and you can export it to Excel, CSV or JSON at any time.
```
