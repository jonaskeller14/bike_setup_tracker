## Description
```text
Hold a part in the garage and drag it onto another bike. The app saves the date of the move, so the part's history shows which bike it was on and since when.

Bike Setup Tracker is free on iOS and Android; the installation history is an option under Settings → Features, and the app suggests it when you move a part. Search "Bike Setup Tracker" in the App Store or on Google Play.
```
