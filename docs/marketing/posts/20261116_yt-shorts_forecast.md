## Description
```text
Say your fork needs a service every 50 hours of riding and you have 14 left. The app predicts the date from your last ten rides on that bike and puts it on your calendar: about six weeks at one ride a week, about three weeks at one every three days. Every new ride updates the prediction, so two extra rides this week bring those six weeks down to about four.

Bike Setup Tracker is free on iOS and Android; riding hours and the prediction come with Strava Sync, an optional paid add-on, and you switch on Tasks and the Calendar under Settings → Features. Search "Bike Setup Tracker" in the App Store or on Google Play.

#mountainbike #bikemaintenance
```
