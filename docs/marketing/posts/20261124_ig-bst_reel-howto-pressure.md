How to check your fork pressure before a ride 🥶

New in 1.8.0: Pressure Check. You set 84 psi on a warm evening. On a 3 °C morning your pump reads 78.5. Start a new setup before the ride, pump back to what you set, and tap the fork to mark it pumped.

Switch it on under Settings → Features.
