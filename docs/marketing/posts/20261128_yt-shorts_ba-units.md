## Description
```text
Your front tire likes 1.5 bar, but your pump reads psi. In a setup, tap the unit next to the pressure and you see the same pressure in psi, just under 22. Pump to 22, type it in psi, and the setup still keeps it in bar. Works for every pressure: psi, bar and kPa. Tap the unit next to sag to type it in mm instead of %.
```
