## Description
```text
Measure the O-ring in millimeters, then in a new setup tap the % next to sag to switch it to mm. Type 32 and you see 20 % right away on a 160 mm fork. Save, done. No travel yet? Open your fork, edit its SAG and type the travel (the stroke for a shock). Forks and shocks from the catalog already have it.
```
